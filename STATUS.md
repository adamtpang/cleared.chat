## Perfect version

Cleared is the private AI inbox for relationship-heavy people: it finds every important open loop, ranks the next conversation, transcribes context, prepares an editable reply, and gets the inbox to zero while the human always performs the final send.

### Company outcome

- **Buyer or user:** Founders, operators, and relationship-heavy professionals with overloaded WhatsApp inboxes.
- **Painful problem:** Unread and reply-owed conversations compete equally, burying promises, money, family, and urgent decisions.
- **Transformation:** A messy WhatsApp inbox becomes a trusted ordered queue that reaches zero without missing a commitment or sending accidentally.
- **Proof that would make this undeniable:** Adam completes repeated measured daily sweeps from a known number of open loops to zero, then one stranger does the same and returns the next week.
