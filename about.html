About cleared.chat

cleared.chat is a private messaging inbox that ranks every open WhatsApp conversation by importance and urgency, then helps draft a reply in the account owner's own voice. A person reviews every draft and makes the final send decision; triage and drafting never send, react, archive or mark messages as read on their own.

Who runs it

cleared.chat is built and operated by Adam Pang, an independent software builder. More about Adam and his other projects is at adampang.com. The full source code is public at github.com/adamtpang/cleared.chat, so anyone can read exactly what the app does with messages.

How cleared.chat works

cleared.chat connects to WhatsApp as a linked device through Baileys, an unofficial WhatsApp Web client, so no separate desktop bridge is needed. Each conversation gets an importance score and an urgency score from 1 to 5, and their product orders the queue. Received voice notes are transcribed with Faster Whisper so their text can inform the next action and draft.

The hosted beta runs at app.cleared.chat with Google sign-in and an isolated worker per account. The self-hosted version runs locally from the public repository. Because Baileys is unofficial, WhatsApp account-policy risk remains, and cleared.chat should never be used for bulk or unsolicited messaging.
