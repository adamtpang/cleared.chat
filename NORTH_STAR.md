# NORTH_STAR: cleared.chat

## North star

Help one person clear every important messaging loop each day by turning a
directly synced WhatsApp inbox into a ranked sequence of tasks, clarifying
questions, and editable drafts. The human always performs the final send.

## Primary buyer

Founders, operators, and relationship-heavy professionals whose WhatsApp inbox
has become an unprioritized task list.

## Painful problem

Every unread or unreplied conversation competes for attention equally, so
important promises, money, family, and time-sensitive decisions are buried.

## Cure

cleared.chat restores a private local inbox, identifies whose turn it is, ranks
open loops by importance x urgency, transcribes voice notes, and gives every
reply-owed chat one clear next action and an editable draft.

## Near-term proof milestone

Within 14 days, record one complete daily sweep where a real user starts with a
measured number of reply-owed chats and reaches zero without an accidental send.

## Binding constraint

Reliable direct sync and complete turn detection are the current constraint.
Distribution comes after the daily loop is trustworthy.
