the method

Not oldest-first. Importance × urgency.

Every chat gets two scores from 1 to 5. Their product (1 to 25) sets the order, so a partner's one-word text outranks a vendor's "URGENT!!!" blast. Then each is sorted into one move.

how it works

Open cleared.chat. Reach zero.

1

Reads everything

Restores your saved WhatsApp session and keeps new messages synced automatically.

WhatsAppvoice notesdirect connection
2

Scores & sorts

Rates each chat importance × urgency (1 to 25) and classifies it: a reply to send, a task to do first, or pure noise.

3

Surfaces the top one

The most important chat first, as a context bundle: who it's from, the gist, what it knows about them, and a reply drafted in your voice.

4

You send

Copy the final draft and send it yourself. The agent never sends, reacts, or archives.

Every chat is a reply to send, or a task to do first.
