How triage works
Importance is 1 to 5 based on the relationship and what is at stake. Urgency is 1 to 5 based on deadlines, waiting, and whether someone is blocked. Priority is importance multiplied by urgency, plus one point for each full week waiting, capped at eight. Direct questions, money, and promises rise. Your last message means waiting. Acknowledgments and unaddressed group chatter are let go.
