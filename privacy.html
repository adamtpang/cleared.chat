Privacy policy

Last updated 2026-11-01. Operator: Adam Pang (adampang.com).

This privacy policy explains what cleared.chat collects on this public website (cleared.chat) and in the hosted beta app (app.cleared.chat), which services process that data, and how to ask for an account to be removed. The self-hosted version runs on your own computer and keeps its data there.

This website (cleared.chat)

  • Hosting: the site is served by Vercel, which processes standard request data such as IP address and user agent to deliver pages.
  • Analytics: PostHog (US cloud) records page views and page leaves, tagged with the site hostname. Automatic click capture, session replay and surveys are turned off in the site code. The PostHog project also has Core Web Vitals (page speed) measurement, heatmaps and dead-click detection enabled, so those run as well and record page speed timings and where clicks land on the page. PostHog keeps an anonymous identifier in your browser to count visits.
  • Fonts: the home page loads the Inter typeface from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google servers when it loads.
  • The website has no forms, no payment processing and no advertising trackers.

The hosted beta (app.cleared.chat)

  • Sign-in: Clerk handles Google sign-in and receives your Google account name and email address.
  • WhatsApp data: after you pair WhatsApp, your linked-device credentials, synced chats, contacts, messages and triage history are kept on the cleared.chat server in an isolated per-account directory. The server is a virtual private server hosted by Hostinger.
  • AI processing: when ranking or drafting runs with an AI key, relevant conversation text is sent to Anthropic's API. Keys saved by an account owner are encrypted before storage. Without an AI key, deterministic offline triage runs on the server instead.
  • Voice notes: received voice notes are transcribed on the cleared.chat server with Faster Whisper. The audio is not sent to a transcription service, and temporary audio files are discarded after transcription.
  • Writing style: learning your writing style is off until you opt in. When enabled, only aggregate habits are kept, never the source messages, and Reset clears the inferred data.

What cleared.chat does not do

cleared.chat does not sell personal data, does not use messages for advertising, and does not send, react to, archive or mark WhatsApp messages as read without a direct click from the account owner.

Account removal and questions

To have a hosted account and its stored WhatsApp data removed, or to ask any privacy question, email adamtpang@gmail.com. You can also unlink cleared.chat at any time from WhatsApp under Linked Devices.
